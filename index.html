Our Philosophy

Cuisine as
Living Art

From hand-dived Hokkaido scallops to white truffles from Périgord — every ingredient is chosen for its singular perfection, never convenience.

The Grand Menu

A Journey of Eight Courses

Our signature tasting menu evolves with the seasons. No two evenings are ever identical.

Course I
Amuse-Bouche

Three bites composed daily from what arrived at dawn — a preview of the chef’s mood and the market’s gift.

Course III
Crustacean & Sea Foam

Brittany lobster with champagne beurre blanc, sea herbs, and a light bisque foam served in a preserved shell.

Course V
The Palate Reset

A single spoonful of lemon verbena granita with pink pepper — designed to cleanse and prepare for what follows.

Course VII
The Cheese Ritual

Eighteen aged farmhouse cheeses from France, wheeled tableside with house-baked walnut bread and quince compote.

Course II
Garden Opening

Young vegetables from our partner farm, dressed with elderflower and wild herb oil harvested that very morning.

Course IV
Terra & Truffle

Hokkaido potato gnocchi with black Périgord truffle shaved tableside, aged parmesan, and a 24-hour consommé.

Course VI
Wagyu A5

Three preparations of Kagoshima A5 wagyu — seared, slow-cooked, and tartare — with bone marrow emulsion.

Course VIII
Grand Dessert

Dark Valrhona chocolate sphere cracked tableside, revealing a warm caramel heart with Tahitian vanilla ice cream.
